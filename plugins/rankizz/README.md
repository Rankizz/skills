# Rankizz

SEO research and tracking: keyword research, SERPs, domain and backlink analysis, site audits, Search Console and GA4 reporting, and App Store research through your Rankizz account.

This plugin connects your agent to the Rankizz remote MCP server at https://www.rankizz.com/mcp and adds 21 skills that tell the agent how to use it: keyword research and clustering, competitor and backlink analysis, technical site audits, Search Console and GA4 analysis, and App Store research.

## Setup

1. Install the plugin in Claude or ChatGPT.
2. Sign in to Rankizz when prompted (OAuth). You need a Rankizz account; research tools use your Rankizz credits.
3. Ask for something like "Research keyword ideas for my project" or "Run a technical audit of my site".

## Data handling

Requests go to https://www.rankizz.com/mcp under your Rankizz account. Rankizz stores your projects, saved keywords and research results as described in the privacy policy: https://www.rankizz.com/privacy. Terms: https://www.rankizz.com/terms. Support: https://www.rankizz.com/support.
